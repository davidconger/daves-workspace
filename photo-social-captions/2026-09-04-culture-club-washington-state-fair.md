# Boy George & Culture Club @ Washington State Fair — Caption Ideas

*Show was Fri, Sep 4, 2026 · Grandstand, Washington State Fair, Puyallup · **opening night of the Fair and the opening night of the 2026 Columbia Bank Concert Series** · special guest Wang Chung · for WARM 106.9 (Adult Contemporary) · shot by @endimages*

You asked whether this opened the Fair's concert series. **It did, and it's better than that** — Friday September 4 was opening night of the Washington State Fair itself *and* the first Grandstand show of the 2026 series. That's two firsts in one night, and it's the news the caption should be built on.

This is also the ideal booking for WARM's audience. An Adult Contemporary station in Seattle has been playing these records for forty years, so unlike the Top 40 posts you don't need to hunt for the one recognizable title. Every Culture Club single is the anchor.

The gift here is **Colour by Numbers**, the 1983 album. It's a complete English phrase, it describes a fairground at night better than anything I could write, and it's their biggest record. The README is right that album titles beat song titles because they tend to be whole thoughts.

---

## ⭐ The pick

> Time (Clock of the Heart) rolled all the way back at @thefair. 🎡 @boygeorgeofficial and @cultureclubofficial opened the Grandstand series in full Colour by Numbers, and Puyallup sang Karma Chameleon like it was 1983. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @endimages)

**Time (Clock of the Heart)** opens the caption doing double duty. It was a US number two off *Kissing to Be Clever*, so it's instantly familiar to this audience, and used as the subject of "rolled all the way back" it describes the entire premise of a legacy act at a state fair. The parenthetical in the real title is what makes it work — you get a clock in the sentence for free.

**Colour by Numbers** is the 1983 album that went UK number one and US number two and sold four million copies in the States. As plain English at a fairground it's doing obvious visual work: the Grandstand lights, the midway, the rides. "In full Colour by Numbers" reads as a description of the night whether or not you know it's an album.

**Karma Chameleon** is the anchor and there's no reason to be clever with it. Number one in the US for three weeks, number one in thirty countries, the best-selling UK single of 1983, seven million copies worldwide. Everyone in that Grandstand knew it. "Sang it like it was 1983" is precise rather than decorative, because *Karma Chameleon* and *Colour by Numbers* are both literally 1983 — the year is accurate, not a vibe.

**#DoThePuyallup** is the strongest hashtag in this whole folder. It's the Fair's sixty-year-old slogan and jingle, it's actively browsed for the full three weeks the Fair runs, and it's local in a way no tour hashtag can be. The US/Canada leg of this tour has no branded hashtag — the UK December run is "The Singles Tour" but that's a different leg and using it here would be wrong. #DoThePuyallup is the better tag anyway. 🔍

## If you want the opening-night news up front

> Opening night of @thefair, opening night of the Grandstand series, and @boygeorgeofficial wasn't going to waste either one. 🎡 @cultureclubofficial turned Puyallup into full Colour by Numbers and forty years fell away somewhere around Karma Chameleon. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @endimages)

The repetition of "opening night" is the point — it's two separate firsts and stacking them makes the fact land. Slightly longer than house style but it reads fast because the clauses are short.

## The miracle version

**It's a Miracle** was a US number thirteen and UK number four off *Colour by Numbers*, and it's a natural English phrase, which the README flags as a free win.

> @boygeorgeofficial and @cultureclubofficial opened @thefair the only way the 80s know how, in full Colour by Numbers. 🎡 Forty years on and Puyallup still sang every word back, which frankly felt like It's a Miracle. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @endimages)

"The only way the 80s know how" gives you the era without having to say "nostalgia," which is a word that slightly insults both the artist and the audience. Use this one if the crowd shots are the story.

## Leaning on the fairground itself

> Forty years of hits and a Ferris wheel behind them. 🎡 @boygeorgeofficial and @cultureclubofficial opened the @thefair Grandstand series in full Colour by Numbers, and Karma Chameleon still stops a crowd cold. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @endimages)

Only run the first line if there's actually a wheel or midway visible in frame. If the shots are all tight on Boy George — and with that wardrobe they usually are — take the pick instead.

## Short, if the photo is a portrait

> Still the best hat at @thefair. 🎡 @boygeorgeofficial and @cultureclubofficial opened the Grandstand series in full Colour by Numbers, forty years after Karma Chameleon and not a step slower. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @endimages)

Boy George's hat is the signature and has been since 1982, so this is affectionate rather than a reach. Confirm he's actually wearing one in the frame you're posting. 😄

## Notes before you post

**Wang Chung was the special guest and needs care.** They opened, they didn't play with Culture Club, and nothing above implies otherwise. If any frame is from their set it needs their handle and its own caption — same rule that applied to Becky G opening for Karol G. Worth knowing that "Everybody Have Fun Tonight" and "Dance Hall Days" are both enormous with WARM's audience, so a separate Wang Chung post has real value rather than being an afterthought. ⚠️

**Do You Really Want to Hurt Me is a trap and I left it out on purpose.** It's their second-biggest song, a US number two, and it is almost impossible to aim at a crowd without it parsing badly. Any construction like "Puyallup really wanted to hurt me" reads as either a complaint about the night or an innuendo, and a station feed can't carry either. Same reasoning that killed *Sin Pijama* on the Karol G post. ⚠️

**Two more to avoid.** **Victims** cannot be pointed at an audience under any circumstances. **Miss Me Blind** is a US number five and tempting, but "Puyallup missed" anything reads as though people didn't show up on opening night, which is the opposite of the story. And per the standing rule, never negate a title — no "no karma, no chameleon" constructions.

**Church of the Poison Mind is fine but weaker.** It was a Top 10 on both sides and it's a great record, but the phrase needs explaining in a caption and the README rule is that anything requiring a footnote has failed. Holding it in reserve in case a second post needs a different title.

**Boy George's solo catalog is available if you need it.** *The Crying Game* and *Everything I Own* are both his and both well known to this audience. Neither fits a celebratory opening-night post, but if a later Fair recap needs a quieter tone they're there.

**The billing is "Boy George & Culture Club."** That's how the Fair advertised it, so both handles belong in the caption. Tagging only the band undersells the draw and tagging only him erases the group — every option above carries both.
