# Cody Johnson @ Tacoma Dome — Caption Ideas

*Show was Sat, Sep 12, 2026 · Tacoma Dome · Cody Johnson Live '26 · for 98.9 The Bull (Country) · shot by @dcongerphoto*

The biggest room of the batch and the deepest catalog. Three Country Airplay number ones, a current album, and a biography that is the real thing rather than a costume — Johnson rode bulls professionally, worked for the Texas Department of Criminal Justice, and became the first unsigned artist to sell out the Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo.

There is also a gift sitting on the new record that is aimed directly at this station, and it would be a waste not to use it.

---

## ⭐ The pick

> He cut a song with Luke Combs called Shoot the Bull, so this one is for @989bull by rights. 🤠 @codyjohnson filled @thetacomadome with The Painter and The Fall, then stood back while Tacoma sang 'Til You Can't at him. (📸 @dcongerphoto)

**Shoot the Bull** is a *Banks of the Trinity* track with Luke Combs, and the pun is free — the station is The Bull, the song is *Shoot the Bull*, and "by rights" is the phrasing that claims it with a wink instead of explaining it. This is the most station-specific caption in the batch and it should run on The Bull's own feed. The handle **@989bull** is verified against the station's own site (23K followers, 2,855 posts); the more obvious **@989thebull is a dormant placeholder with 6 followers and zero posts**, so do not let autocomplete pick it. ⚠️

**'Til You Can't** is the anchor and it belongs at the end. Number one in 2022, multi-platinum, and comfortably his best-known song — a Bull listener recognizes it immediately, which per the anchor rule gives the other two titles permission to be titles.

**"Stood back while Tacoma sang it at him"** is deliberate and it matters. *'Til You Can't* is a song about doing the thing while you still have the chance, built on his grandfather's watch. It is the emotional peak of the set, not the rowdy one. Writing "loud enough to shake the Dome" would have been the easy line and it would have mischaracterized the song. The crowd singing it back while he lets them is both more accurate and a better image.

**The Painter** was a number one in 2024 and **The Fall** went number one in May 2026, which makes it the current single — naming both signals that this is a 2026 show rather than a greatest-hits recap. They also sit next to each other cleanly because both are short "The" titles.

**#HORNSUP is the one hashtag available here, and it belongs to the station.** ⚠️ The tour is branded **Cody Johnson Live '26**, which does not compress into anything anyone is actually typing, and no official tour hashtag exists — his fan club branding is "COJO Nation," which is not a tour tag. But 98.9 The Bull publishes **#HORNSUP** in its own Instagram bio, and on a Cody Johnson post it reads as both the station's tag and a perfectly natural crowd gesture. Add it after the last title if this runs on the station feed. Do not invent a tour hashtag.

## The bull rider one

This is the fact most people at the show do not know, and it is the strongest angle he has.

> Before the number ones, @codyjohnson rode bulls for a living and was the first unsigned artist to sell out the Houston rodeo. 🤠 Twenty years later he filled @thetacomadome and got 'Til You Can't sung back to him by every row. (📸 @dcongerphoto)

Both claims are verified and both are unusual enough to stop a scroll. "Every row" is the right crowd-size flourish for a dome — it describes scale without inventing an attendance number. ⚠️ Sanity-check "twenty years" against whatever date you want to anchor it to, or cut the number and write "years later," which is safe and nearly as good.

---

## The Texas one

*Banks of the Trinity* is named for the Trinity River near Sebastopol, Texas, where he grew up. That is a real story and it is the thing he has been talking about all year.

> From the banks of the Trinity to the floor of @thetacomadome. 🤠 @codyjohnson played The Fall, Dirt Cheap and a 'Til You Can't that nobody got through dry, and Tacoma gave him the whole night back. (📸 @dcongerphoto)

The album title works here as plain geography in the first clause, which is the same trick as *Kansas Anymore* on the Role Model post — the reader does not need to know it is a record for the sentence to land. **Dirt Cheap** peaked at five and is an easy add. Three titles is the ceiling and this uses all three, so it needs a wide frame to justify itself.

## Short

> @codyjohnson and a full @thetacomadome on 'Til You Can't. 🤠 That is the whole post. (📸 @dcongerphoto)

One title, one venue, no explanation. "That is the whole post" only works when the photograph genuinely is the whole post, so hold it for the best frame of the night.

## The Carrie Underwood option

**I'm Gonna Love You** is his duet with Carrie Underwood and it peaked at three. She was not there, so this needs careful phrasing.

> The Carrie Underwood duet hits differently with fifteen thousand people covering her part. 🤠 @codyjohnson brought I'm Gonna Love You and The Painter to @thetacomadome, and 'Til You Can't closed it out. (📸 @dcongerphoto)

The crowd-covers-the-absent-guest observation is true at basically every show that plays a duet, and it neatly avoids implying she appeared. ⚠️ Same caution as above on the number — replace fifteen thousand with a real figure or cut it.

## Notes before you post

**Do not connect him to the rodeo at the Fair.** ⚠️ There is a PRCA rodeo running in Puyallup the same week and Johnson is a former professional bull rider, which makes this an easy and very wrong sentence to write. He played the Tacoma Dome, an indoor arena thirty minutes away, on his own tour. His rodeo history is Texas and Houston, not Puyallup. Keep the two stories apart.

**Travelin' Soldier is his highest-charting song and it is still not the anchor.** It reached number 12 on the Hot 100, above anything else he has done, but it is a Dixie Chicks cover and a war song about a death. It is the wrong emotional register for a celebratory concert photo, and crediting him with a song most people associate with somebody else muddies the post. Left out on purpose.

**Human is available and underused.** It peaked at eight and the title is a single clean word, which makes it unusually easy to slip into a sentence. Nothing above needed it, but it is the first thing I would reach for on a second post from this night.

**Fool Proof is the other new collaboration**, with Brothers Osborne, off the same album. No pun available for this station, so *Shoot the Bull* got the slot, but it is there if a different outlet needs a *Banks of the Trinity* reference.

**Album and tour are not the same name here.** The record is *Banks of the Trinity*, released June 26, 2026. The tour is *Cody Johnson Live '26*. Do not write "the Banks of the Trinity Tour" — it does not exist, and that specific mistake is circulating.

**Terri Clark and David Lewis opened.** Confirmed on both the Tacoma Dome event page and his own tour listing. Terri Clark is a genuine draw for this audience — a Canadian Country Music Hall of Famer with her own run of nineties hits — so tagging her is worth a repost if you shot her set. None of the options above mention the openers, because the Dome show is Johnson's story, but a separate openers post is the better use of that material.

**The venue handle is @thetacomadome, not @tacomadome.** ⚠️ The short version is not the venue. The correct account is linked from tacomadome.org and its bio reads *"The official Instagram of the 'Dome of Our Own'."* No blue check, which is why it is worth stating plainly here.
