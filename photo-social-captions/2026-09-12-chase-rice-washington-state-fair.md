# Chase Rice @ Washington State Fair — Caption Ideas

*Show was Sat, Sep 12, 2026 · **Dancin' in the Dirt**, Columbia Bank Grandstand arena floor, Washington State Fair, Puyallup · follows the PRCA Cinch Playoff Wild Card rodeo · for 98.9 The Bull (Country) · shot by @dcongerphoto*

Third and final Dancin' in the Dirt night, and the strongest catalog of the three. Rice has two Country Airplay number ones and a platinum single, so unlike the Marlowe post there is no anchor anxiety here — the problem is choosing.

He also has an album title that lands on this specific floor better than anything else in the batch, and one album title that must be kept a long way away from it.

---

## ⭐ The pick

> The rodeo cleared out, the dirt stayed put, and @chaserice turned the floor at @wastatefairec into the closest thing Puyallup gets to a Dirt Road Communion. 🤠 Ready Set Roll into Eyes On You, and the whole fair came with him. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @dcongerphoto)

**Dirt Road Communion** is his 2012 album, and it is doing something none of the other titles in this batch can: it describes the event. Dancin' in the Dirt is a crowd standing shoulder to shoulder on a rodeo floor at nine at night, which is a communion on a dirt road in every sense but the literal one. "The closest thing Puyallup gets to" is the hedge that makes it a joke rather than an overclaim.

**"The rodeo cleared out, the dirt stayed put"** is the best opening line across the three Fair files. It is six words of scene-setting, it is exactly true, and it explains the entire concept of the event to anyone who has never been. Short clauses, no em dashes, reads fast.

**Eyes On You** is the anchor. Number one on Country Airplay, his biggest song, and the one a Bull listener will recognize instantly. It also has the useful property of being a phrase you can point at a crowd without it getting weird.

**Ready Set Roll** is platinum and peaked at five, and putting it before *Eyes On You* gives the sentence an actual shape — the rowdy one first, the big one second, and "the whole arena came with him" as the payoff. Two titles plus an album title is right at the ceiling, which is why nothing else got in.

## The Chute version

**Drinkin' Beer. Talkin' God. Amen.**, his 2020 number one with Florida Georgia Line, has an almost unfair amount of overlap with this event: the Dancin' in the Dirt ticket comes with a 21+ bar called **The Chute** that opens at 7:30 and looks out onto the rodeo.

> There is a bar called The Chute, a floor made of rodeo dirt, and a guy with a number one called Drinkin' Beer. Talkin' God. Amen. 🤠 @wastatefairec did not have to try very hard on this one. @chaserice closed it out with Eyes On You. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @dcongerphoto)

"@wastatefairec did not have to try very hard on this one" is the joke, and the three-item list before it is the setup. The song title's own punctuation makes it a mouthful, so it has to be the last item in the list where the rhythm can absorb it.

## The résumé one

His pre-music biography is genuinely unusual and completely verifiable: **linebacker at North Carolina, then a NASCAR pit crew job with Hendrick Motorsports as a rear tire carrier, then runner-up on Survivor: Nicaragua.**

> Played linebacker at UNC, carried tires for Hendrick Motorsports, finished second on Survivor. 🤠 Now @chaserice gets an arena floor at @wastatefairec to himself, and Ready Set Roll hits different when you know the guy actually used to. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @dcongerphoto)

The pit crew detail and *Ready Set Roll* pair up on their own, which is the only reason this one earns its length. Three biographical clauses is a lot of caption before the photo gets mentioned, so use this only on a strong full-stage frame where people will stay for it.

## Short

> @chaserice, an arena floor full of dirt, and roughly four thousand people with Eyes On You. 🤠 Dancin' in the Dirt at @wastatefairec. #DoThePuyallup (📸 @dcongerphoto)

The title used as a pun on the crowd watching him is the whole idea, and it is the kind of thing that works better short than explained. ⚠️ Swap "roughly four thousand" for a real number or cut it — I have not verified Dancin' in the Dirt's floor capacity, and a made-up attendance figure is exactly the sort of detail someone in the comments will know.

## Notes before you post

**I Hate Cowboys cannot appear anywhere on this post.** ⚠️ *I Hate Cowboys & All Dogs Go to Hell* is his 2023 album and a perfectly good record, but this audience just spent an evening watching the PRCA Cinch Playoff Wild Card rodeo. Putting "I Hate Cowboys" on a photo taken on that dirt, on a country station's feed, aimed at people who paid to watch cowboys, is the single worst available move in this entire batch. It is the one thing in these five files I would call a genuine unforced error, so it is flagged loudly.

**Chase Rice was never a bull rider.** ⚠️ Worth stating plainly because it is an easy assumption to make given the setting. The rodeo résumé belongs to **Cody Johnson**, who is at the Tacoma Dome the same night and really did ride bulls professionally. The irony is nice and you can enjoy it privately, but do not write a caption that gives Rice a rodeo past he does not have. His actual background — football, pit crew, Survivor — is strange enough to be more interesting anyway, which is why it got its own option above.

**Bad Day to Be a Cold Beer is tempting and slightly weak.** It is a fun title that fits a fair crowd, but it missed the top twenty, so it does not carry the recognition to justify a slot when *Eyes On You* and *Drinkin' Beer. Talkin' God. Amen.* are both sitting there at number one. Left out on chart grounds, not taste.

**Lonely If You Are is the same call.** Peaked at nineteen, real song, but "lonely" is the wrong word to put near a photograph of a packed arena floor.

**He co-wrote Cruise.** Florida Georgia Line's diamond-certified monster, and one of the best credentials in modern country. None of the captions use it because it points at somebody else's song, but it is the strongest fact in reserve if a follow-up post needs an angle nothing above has used.

**Do not name his tour, because nobody can verify what it is called.** ⚠️ His own tour page renders via JavaScript and publishes no tour title. Searches confidently return two *different* names — "Go Down Singin' International Tour 2026" and "ELDORA Tour" — and both trace back to **chaserice.org**, which is a ticket-resale lookalike of his real site, **chaserice.com**. Neither is trustworthy. Write "Chase Rice" or "in support of ELDORA," never a tour name.

**ELDORA is the current album and it is missing from every option above.** It is his eighth record, written in Colorado, sparked by a Red Rocks show, produced with Oscar Charles, and released now that he is fully independent. The captions lean on *Dirt Road Communion* (2012) because that title fits the dirt floor and ELDORA does not — but if the post needs to feel current rather than clever, swapping in "his first record as an independent artist" is an easy and accurate upgrade. His Instagram bio currently reads *"Connie Lou out now!"*, so that is the track being pushed this week.

**The Fair's handle is @wastatefairec, and this one was wrong until now.** ⚠️ The obvious `@thefair` belongs to a private individual with 119 followers and one post. The real account is **@wastatefairec** — verified, 34K followers, bio begins "Official account," linked from the footer of thefair.com. The "ec" is for Washington State Fair Event Center, the org's formal name. Earlier posts in this folder used `@thefair` and have been corrected; if any of them are already live, edit the caption.

**Tag @puyalluprodeo too.** The PRCA Cinch Playoffs Wild Card at Puyallup Rodeo runs September 10-13 and its account's own bio points at @wastatefairec. On a Dancin' in the Dirt post you are standing on their dirt, so tagging them is both accurate and an easy repost.
