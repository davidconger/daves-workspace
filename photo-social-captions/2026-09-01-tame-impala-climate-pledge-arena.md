## ⭐ The pick

> @tameimpala turned the End of Summer into the first of two sold out nights at @climatepledgearena. 🌀 Seattle is still Lost in Yesterday, and the Currents run again tonight. #TheDeadbeatTour (📸 @dcongerphoto)

This one is doing four jobs at once and none of them are forced.

**End of Summer** is the gift of the calendar. It's the lead single off *Deadbeat*, it won Best Dance/Electronic Recording at the 2026 Grammys, so it's the current, relevant, award-winning song people are actually streaming right now. And you shot it on September 1. It is literally the end of summer in Seattle. That's the same kind of luck as the orange crowd at Karol G, where the song title just happens to describe the real night. 🌀

**Lost in Yesterday** solves the "a look back at last night" problem better than the plain phrase did. It's a single off *The Slow Rush*, and the song is about the way memory sweetens a night after the fact, which is exactly what a next-day photo post is. The title is doing your framing for you instead of you having to say "a look back."

**Currents** is the 2015 album, and the venue pun is real rather than a stretch. Climate Pledge Arena is the first net zero carbon certified arena in the world, so it runs on renewable electricity. "The Currents run again tonight" reads as power, as electricity, and as the album, and it also does the work of telling people there's a second show. That's the second-night tease built into a title.

## If you want the big recognizable song in it

**The Less I Know the Better** is the anchor if you want a title a casual listener definitely knows. It's past two billion streams and it won Triple J's Hottest 100 of the 2010s outright. It never was a Hot 100 hit, which is worth knowing, but by streaming and cultural reach it is far and away their signature song, and a Top 40 audience will recognize it instantly.

> The Less I Know the Better, the more @tameimpala proved otherwise at @climatepledgearena. 💜 🌀 Night one of two sold out, and Seattle is still Lost in Yesterday. #TheDeadbeatTour (📸 @dcongerphoto)

That first clause plays the title against itself, which is the kind of double meaning that lands. Fair warning that it's the most "written" of these options, so if you want it to feel effortless, take the pick instead.

## Leaning into night two

Since the second show is tonight, you can make the post do promotional work:

> @tameimpala made the End of Summer last one more day. 🌀 Night one of two sold out at @climatepledgearena, and there's still time to Let It Happen tonight. #TheDeadbeatTour (📸 @dcongerphoto)

**Let It Happen** is the *Currents* opener and one of the two songs that hit the Triple J top five in 2015, so it carries real recognition. Used this way it's an invitation rather than a description, which is a nice change of gear from the other options.

## Notes before you post

**Verify two handles.** I'd expect **@tameimpala** and **@climatepledgearena**, but confirm both. Wikipedia doesn't carry socials and a wrong tag is the hardest thing to undo after posting. 🔍

**Dominic Fike is the special guest on this tour.** None of the captions above mention him, which is correct for a Tame Impala post, but if any of your frames are actually of his set, that post needs his handle instead. Don't let an opener's photo run under a headliner's caption.

**Titles to leave alone tonight.** **Loser** and **Deadbeat** are both real and both current, but a caption calling anyone a loser or a deadbeat will get misread at a glance, and a radio feed can't afford that. **Dracula** is a genuine *Deadbeat* single but it will read as a Halloween post in early September. **Feels Like We Only Go Backwards** is tempting for a look-back caption, but it's about a relationship stalling out, so pointing it at the show implies the show regressed. Skip it. ⚠️

**One structural note.** Every option keeps "first of two sold out nights" or "night one of two" intact, because that's the actual news in your original line and it's the part that makes people click. The song titles replaced the decorative words, not the informative ones.
