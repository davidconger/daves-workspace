# Dave Matthews Band @ The Gorge Amphitheatre — Caption Ideas

*Show was Fri, Sep 4, 2026 · The Gorge Amphitheatre, George, WA · first of three nights (Sep 4/5/6) · the closing run of the 2026 Summer Tour · openers Infinity Song and Stephen Wilson Jr. · for WARM 106.9 (Adult Contemporary) · shot by @xanderramone*

Two facts carry this post and neither one is generic hype.

**These three nights end the whole tour.** The Gorge run isn't a stop on the 2026 Summer Tour, it's the finale of it. Whatever happens on Sunday is the last thing this band does all year.

**It's Labor Dave Weekend.** That's the actual fan name for it, not a pun I invented, and the tradition goes back to their first Gorge show in 1996 and their first three-night run in 2001. It is the longest-standing artist residency at any amphitheatre in the country.

Because this is night one of three, the caption should do what the Tame Impala one did with "the Currents run again tonight" — tell people there's more coming. DMB handed us the perfect title for that.

---

## ⭐ The pick

> @davematthewsband turned Labor Dave Weekend into the first of three nights at @gorgeamphitheatre. 🌄 Two decades on, that hillside Away from the World is still the Best of What's Around, and Come Tomorrow they do it all again. #DaveMatthewsBand (📸 @xanderramone)

Three titles, and each one is describing something literally true.

**Away from the World** is the 2012 album, and it is a plain description of where you were standing. The Gorge is in George, Washington, two and a half hours east of Seattle, surrounded by farmland, on a cliff above the Columbia River. There is no city anywhere near it. That title is doing real geographic work rather than being dropped in as a reference, which is the difference between the Noah Kahan caption landing and the early drafts that didn't.

**Best of What's Around** is the opening track of *Under the Table and Dreaming*, the 1994 debut. The Gorge is routinely called the most beautiful amphitheatre in America, and the phrase reads simultaneously as the view, as the band's standing at that venue, and as a deep-catalog title that longtime fans will clock instantly. It's a 1994 track rather than a radio hit, which is fine — the README rule is that only one title has to be radio-familiar, and *Come Tomorrow* plus the band's name carries that load.

**Come Tomorrow** is the 2018 album and title track, and it is the whole reason this caption works. Read as English it means "when tomorrow comes." Read as a title it's their most recent number one album. And it announces night two without a single word of promo language. That's the second-night tease built into a title, exactly like *Currents* did at Climate Pledge.

**"Two decades on"** is deliberately vague. They've played the Gorge since 1996 and run three nights since 2001, but the consecutive-year count gets reported inconsistently once you account for 2020 being cancelled. A soft phrase is defensible; a specific number is a correction waiting to happen. 🔍

## If you want to lead with the tour finale

The fact that this closes out their entire year is genuinely newsworthy and almost nobody in the comments will know it.

> The last three nights of @davematthewsband's entire summer, and they're spending them at @gorgeamphitheatre. 🌄 Night one of three above the Columbia, where it's always been the Best of What's Around. Come Tomorrow, they go again. #DaveMatthewsBand (📸 @xanderramone)

Front-loads the news, which is the move that made the original Tame Impala line work before any wordplay went into it. Good under a wide crowd-and-canyon shot.

## The radio-familiar version

If WARM's audience needs the anchor hit rather than deep catalog, this is the one. **Crash Into Me** is from *Crash* (1996) and it is the band's defining Adult Contemporary record — for an AC station it's a stronger anchor than anything off the newer albums.

> Nineteen ninety-six called and @davematthewsband still knows exactly what to do with a sunset. 🌄 Night one of three at @gorgeamphitheatre, the Columbia below and the whole hillside ready to Crash Into Me. #DaveMatthewsBand (📸 @xanderramone)

Careful with this one: it commits to a sunset. Only run it if the frames actually show one. Night shows at the Gorge start before sundown so it's likely, but "likely" is how the rain-pun mistake happened.

## Notes before you post

**The hashtag is the weak link, and you should know that going in.** DMB did not brand the 2026 Summer Tour with a hashtag the way Noah Kahan had #TheGreatDivideTour or Tame Impala had #TheDeadbeatTour. There is no official equivalent to reach for. I've used **#DaveMatthewsBand** because it's guaranteed correct and actively browsed. **#LaborDave** and **#DMBGorge** are real fan tags with genuine traffic and would be better discovery channels if you're willing to use a fan tag rather than an official one — worth testing, since the README notes the tour-hashtag post was the best performer. ⚠️

**Don't claim sold out unless you've confirmed this night.** The Gorge run sells out essentially every year and it would probably be true, but "probably true" isn't the standard, and it's the kind of detail people correct in the comments. None of the options above assert it. If you confirm it, "night one of three sold out" drops into any of them cleanly.

**Infinity Song and Stephen Wilson Jr. opened Friday.** They're not mentioned, which is right for a DMB post, but if any frame in the carousel is from either set, that photo needs its own handle. This is the Becky G and Karol G lesson — an opener's photo cannot run under a headliner's caption.

**The Gorge is not Seattle.** Every previous caption in this folder could say "Seattle" because every show was in Seattle. This one wasn't. It's in George, Washington, and the crowd comes from all over the Northwest and well beyond. Saying Seattle sang anything here is just factually wrong, and Gorge regulars are exactly the audience that would notice.

**Titles to leave alone.** **Don't Drink the Water** is a real and well-known song, but the Gorge in early September is genuinely hot and dehydration is a live safety issue there — a station feed telling people not to drink water is indefensible even as a joke. ⚠️ **Busted Stuff** and **Grey Street** both read as complaints about the night. **Crash** on its own near any travel or driving context is a bad glance-read. **Ants Marching** is safe and vivid for a packed-lawn shot if you want another option, though it doesn't carry meaning the way the three in the pick do.

**Why no "The Space Between" or "Where Are You Going."** Both are legitimate AC hits and both were on the table. *The Space Between* is about distance in a relationship, so aiming it at a crowd implies disconnection. *Where Are You Going* asks a question the post shouldn't be asking on night one of three — the answer you want is "back here tomorrow," which is what *Come Tomorrow* says outright and better.
